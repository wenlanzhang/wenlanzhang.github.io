Wenlan Zhang is a PhD researcher at the Bartlett Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis (CASA), University College London, and a part-time Research Assistant at the Leverhulme Centre for Demographic Science, University of Oxford.

Her research examines how digital and geospatial data represent people and places, and which populations may be systematically missed, particularly in rapidly urbanising and data-scarce settings. Her PhD, “Quantifying Digital Injustice: The Utility of Digital Footprint Data in the Global South,” develops computational and spatial approaches to identify, quantify and mitigate representation biases in emerging data sources.

Her broader research spans urban data science, population and mobility measurement, spatial inequality, and machine learning, with a particular interest in translating new forms of digital data into more equitable evidence for urban and development policy.

Her PhD is supervised by Prof. Chen Zhong, Prof. Mark Pelling, and Dr. Faith Taylor.

Feel free to get in touch to discuss research collaborations and related opportunities.

她的研究关注数字与地理空间数据如何呈现城市中的人口与空间，以及哪些群体可能被系统性遗漏，尤其聚焦于快速城市化及数据相对匮乏的地区。她的博士研究 “Quantifying Digital Injustice: The Utility of Digital Footprint Data in the Global South” 通过空间分析、机器学习和计算方法，识别、量化并缓解新型数字数据中的代表性偏差。

她目前的研究兴趣涵盖城市数据科学、人口与流动性测度、空间不平等及机器学习，并关注如何将新型数字数据转化为更公平、更可靠的城市与发展政策证据。
