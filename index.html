About

简介

Her PhD, “Quantifying Digital Injustice: The Utility of Digital Footprint Data in the Global South,” examines how digital and geospatial data represent people and places, and which populations may be systematically missed, using computational and spatial approaches to identify, quantify, and mitigate these biases.

More broadly, her work spans urban data science, population and mobility measurement, spatial inequality, and machine learning, with a focus on using emerging forms of digital data and AI to support more robust and equitable evidence for policy-making.

Her PhD is supervised by Prof. Chen Zhong, Prof. Mark Pelling, and Dr. Faith Taylor.

她的博士研究 “Quantifying Digital Injustice: The Utility of Digital Footprint Data in the Global South” 关注数字与地理空间数据如何呈现人群与地方，以及哪些群体可能被系统性遗漏，并通过计算与空间方法识别、量化并缓解这些偏差。

更广泛地，她的研究涵盖城市数据科学、人口与流动性测度、空间不平等及机器学习，关注如何利用新型数字数据与人工智能，为政策制定提供更稳健、更公平的证据。

博士导师为钟晨教授、Mark Pelling 教授和Faith Taylor 博士。
