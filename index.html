About

简介

I am a spatial data scientist working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, geospatial data, and urban development. My work explores how emerging data and computational methods can help us better understand cities and support evidence-based urban planning and policy, particularly in the Global South.

My research combines machine learning, computer vision, remote sensing, and spatial analysis with diverse sources of urban data to study land use, population, mobility, infrastructure, environmental conditions, and spatial inequality.

My PhD at UCL CASA examines data injustice in urban policymaking: who and what may be missing from increasingly data-driven representations of cities, how these gaps affect urban indicators and decisions, and how they can be identified and mitigated.

我是一名空间数据科学家，工作位于人工智能、地理空间数据与城市发展的交叉领域。我的研究探索新兴数据与计算方法如何帮助我们更好地理解城市，并支持循证的城市规划与政策，尤其是在全球南方。

我的研究将机器学习、计算机视觉、遥感与空间分析相结合，运用多元城市数据研究土地利用、人口、流动性、基础设施、环境状况与空间不平等。

我在 UCL CASA 的博士研究关注城市政策制定中的数据不公：在日益数据驱动的城市表征中，谁与什么可能被遗漏，这些缺口如何影响城市指标与决策，以及如何识别并缓解这些问题。
